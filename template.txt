ABOUT HARYANA YUVA SAMWAD
The inaugural edition of Haryana Yuva Samvad 2025, organized by Rajdhani Yuva Sansad, was convened on 2nd–3rd April 2025 at the historic Haryana Vidhan Sabha, marking the launch of a transformative youth parliamentary initiative in the state. The conference brought together 65 students from across the country, chosen for their leadership potential, policy awareness, communication skills, and commitment to public service, forming a diverse delegation unified by the goal of engaging with real-world governance challenges. Over two days, participants participated in focused committee deliberations on key agendas including E-Governance & E-Literacy along with Women Empowerment, working collectively to develop innovative, inclusive, and sustainable solutions for Haryana's progress. The success of the inaugural edition was made possible through the unwavering support of the Secretariat of the Haryana Vidhan Sabha and the guidance of the Hon'ble Speaker, Shri Harvinder Kalyan Ji, whose encouragement lent institutional strength to the initiative, firmly establishing Haryana Yuva Samvad as a credible platform for youth-led policy discourse and future leadership development.
Building upon the success of its inaugural edition, the Second Edition of Haryana Yuva Samvad was hosted by the Haryana Vidhan Sabha, in association with Rajdhani Yuva Sansad, on 16th–17th January 2026 at the historic premises of the Haryana Legislative Assembly. By opening the doors of one of the state's foremost democratic institutions to aspiring young leaders, the Haryana Vidhan Sabha reaffirmed its dedication to promoting constitutional awareness, legislative understanding, and meaningful engagement with public policy. The conference brought together over 60 carefully selected delegates from educational institutions across India through a rigorous and merit-based selection process. The successful conduct of the conference was made possible through the unwavering support of the Secretariat of the Haryana Vidhan Sabha and the visionary guidance of the Hon'ble Speaker, Shri Harvinder Kalyan Ji.

The procedure for participation is as follows:
Candidates must submit a Statement of Purpose (SOP) for the initial screening round.
Shortlisted candidates will advance to the evaluation stage, where they will be required to prepare and deliver a speech on one of the assigned agendas.
Candidates may present their speech either in favor of or in opposition of the agenda.
Candidates must also be prepared to face cross-questioning based on their delivered speech, conference agenda and general knowledge topics.
The speech must be strictly original, non-plagiarized, and free of any AI-generated content.
The maximum time limit is 1 minute and 30 seconds.
